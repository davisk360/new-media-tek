Hi [Partner Name],

I wanted to share some clarity I gained after researching vendor registration and procurement portals for our B2B strategy.


THE KEY INSIGHT

Vendor portal registration is NOT required to win private sector B2B clients. Here's the breakdown:


GOVERNMENT CONTRACTS (Long Game)

- SAM.gov registration, NAICS codes, and state/local vendor portals are mandatory only for government contracting
- This is a 6-12 month+ process before seeing RFP opportunities
- Worth pursuing eventually, but not our immediate revenue path


CORPORATE VENDOR PORTALS (Payment, Not Sales)

- Portals like Ariba, Coupa, and Jaggaer are payment systems, not job boards
- Companies use these after you've already won the contract
- Registration happens when a client requests it for invoicing purposes
- You don't browse these looking for work—they find you


WHAT THIS MEANS FOR US

For private sector B2B, we don't need to wait on any registration. We need:

1. A professional website (Done)
2. Basic business setup – LLC, EIN, business bank account
3. Professional liability insurance (E&O) – $1-2M coverage
4. W-9 and COI ready to send on request
5. Direct outreach – LinkedIn, referrals, networking, content marketing


RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS

1. Direct Outreach – Target companies with legacy .NET systems via LinkedIn Sales Navigator
2. Content Marketing – Publish technical insights to establish authority (blog is live)
3. Referral Network – Reach out to former colleagues and contacts
4. Clutch/UpCity Profiles – Get listed on B2B software directories
5. LinkedIn Company Page – Optimize for enterprise .NET keywords

The vendor portals and government registration can be a parallel track for long-term positioning, but our immediate focus should be direct B2B sales.
