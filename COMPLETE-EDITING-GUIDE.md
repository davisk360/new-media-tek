## 🎨 **How to Edit - STEP BY STEP**

### **Method 1: Click-to-Edit (Most Common)**
1. **Navigate to the page** in the sidebar
2. **Click any field** you want to edit
3. **Type your changes** in the popup editor
4. **Click Save (✓)** to confirm or **Cancel (X)** to discard
5. **See changes immediately** in the interface

### **Method 2: Project Management (Portfolio Only)**
1. **Go to Portfolio page**
2. **Click "Add Project"** for new projects
3. **Edit inline** - Click directly in project fields
4. **Remove projects** - Click trash icon (🗑️)

### **Method 3: Add New Pages**
1. **Click ➕ button** next to "Website Pages"
2. **Fill in page details**
3. **Click "Create Page"**
4. **Edit the new page** like any other

## 📝 **Field Types & What They Mean**

### **Heading Fields**
- **Use for**: Page titles, section headings
- **Appearance**: Large, bold text on website
- **Examples**: "Enterprise .NET Development", "About Our Team"

### **Text Fields**
- **Use for**: Short phrases, subtitles, single lines
- **Appearance**: Regular text on website
- **Examples**: "Senior-led team", "Fortune 500 Success Stories"

### **Textarea Fields**
- **Use for**: Paragraphs, detailed descriptions
- **Appearance**: Multi-line text content
- **Examples**: Full bios, service descriptions, project details

### **Email Fields**
- **Use for**: Email addresses only
- **Appearance**: Clickable email links
- **Examples**: "contact@newmediatek.net"

### **Phone Fields**
- **Use for**: Phone numbers only
- **Appearance**: Clickable phone links
- **Examples**: "+1 (555) 123-4567"
