# 🎬 Live Preview Feature - Complete Guide

## 🎯 **NEW: Real-Time Visual Feedback**

Your Visual CMS now includes a **Live Preview panel** that shows your changes **instantly as you type**! No more guessing what your content will look like - see it in real-time.

---

## ✨ **What the Live Preview Does**

### 🟢 **Real-Time Updates**
- **Instant feedback** as you type
- **Visual highlighting** of currently editing fields
- **Live content rendering** with proper formatting
- **Sticky positioning** - always visible while you edit

### 🎨 **Visual Indicators**
- **Green pulsing dot** - Shows live preview is active
- **Yellow highlighting** - Field being edited
- **Professional layout** - Mirrors your website structure
- **Smart formatting** - Headings, paragraphs, and text display correctly

---

## 🚀 **How to Use Live Preview**

### **Step 1: Enable Live Preview**
1. **Look for the "Live Preview" button** in the header (green when active)
2. **Click the button** to toggle the preview panel on/off
3. **See the panel appear** on the right side of your screen

### **Step 2: Start Editing**
1. **Click any field** in the main editing area
2. **Start typing** your changes
3. **Watch the preview update instantly** on the right
4. **See yellow highlighting** around the field being edited

### **Step 3: Review Your Changes**
1. **Toggle preview mode** (eye icon) to see full-page preview
2. **Compare live preview** with full preview
3. **Save your changes** when satisfied
